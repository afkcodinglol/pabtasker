Planner settings

Applies to Autofill, Optimize and Generate.
Alternate training skills
Never put two training tasks of the same skill back to back when another task can go between them, and space repeat visits to a skill as far apart as possible. Requirements and total time are unaffected.
Quest XP before training
When a quest needs a level, first do available quests that reward XP in that skill if they save more time than they cost.
Gather quest items (ironman)
Puts ACQUIRE:Item,qty-qty tasks right before each quest for the items the backend can gather. If the account is missing the levels to gather them (for example 15 Mining for iron ore), that training is planned before the quest, unless it would take more than about 5 hours. Tools are gathered once.
Quest-first strength
How much training time a quest (with its prerequisites and any small extra training it needs) must save before it is done for its XP or lamps instead of training.
Train recommended stats
Treat the backend's recommended levels (43 Prayer, 20 HP and similar) as requirements.
Allow partner quests
Shield of Arrav and Heroes' Quest need websocket matchmaking.
Use Wintertodt
Train Firemaking 50+ with MINIGAME:WINTERTODT.
Start fresh ironmen with IRON_STARTER_PART1
When the list is empty and the start is a fresh ironman, the first autofill begins with the starter route.
